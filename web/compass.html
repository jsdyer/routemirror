What the rose measures

Each segment between consecutive points contributes its length to the nearest of eight compass axes. A train travelling east and the same path travelling west both count toward E–W. Each selected shape ID counts once, regardless of how often trips use it. Distinct shape IDs can trace the same corridor, so overlaps and route variants can contribute repeatedly.

Use rail feeds to explore the alignment of published train routes or a bus feed to explore the alignment of bus paths. These are scheduled route geometries. The rose does not measure track inventory, ridership or service frequency.
